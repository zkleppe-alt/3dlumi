Phone: the left stick walks, the right stick (👁) turns the view — or drag the screen; pinch to zoom. In a fight, tap the right stick to lock on to a foe (tap again to let go). The gold button appears near people, signs, doors and stairs. ☰ opens the menu.

Mouse & keyboard: click the game to steer the view with the mouse (Esc frees the cursor). WASD to walk, mouse to look, scroll to zoom. Left click swings (the third swing is the Ground Pound) and reads on in a conversation; hold right click (or Shift) to guard; Space to jump (up onto a gallery's edge, or down off one); Alt or C to dodge-roll the way you're moving; Q or middle click to lock on (flick the mouse to switch foes); E to talk or enter; 1 for a potion; R to mount; M for the menu. Change any key, the look speed and invert in ☰ System → Keyboard Controls. In contests: Space or J strikes or urges, Shift guards or jumps.

Controller: plug one in and press a button. Left stick walks, right stick looks. Ⓐ talks (or jumps), Ⓑ dodges, Ⓧ/RT swings, LB guards, RB locks on, Start is the menu (full list in ☰ → Controls).

Every street, house and hall comes from your index.html.
