# Luminar 3D — Kingdom's Last Knight

**▶ Play: https://zkleppe-alt.github.io/3dlumi/**

Ride out from Luminar, cleanse the ten chapels and free their generals. Runs in the browser — no install.

**Controls**
- Keyboard & mouse: WASD to walk, click the game to steer the view with the mouse (Esc frees it), left click to swing, hold right click to guard, Space to jump, Alt/C to dodge, E to talk, Q to lock on, M for the menu. Rebind anything in ☰ → Controls.
- Controller: plug one in and press a button. Left stick walks, right stick looks, Ⓐ talks/jumps, Ⓑ dodges, Ⓧ/RT swings, LB guards, RB locks on, Start is the menu.
- Phone/tablet: on-screen sticks and buttons.

Your progress saves in your browser on this site.
